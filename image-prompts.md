## Global Style Rules

Every prompt should include these baseline instructions:

```
Style: Hyper-realistic photograph, natural daylight, Australian suburban setting.
People: Two male plumbers (one ~60s with grey hair, stocky build — "Steve"; one ~30s, athletic — "Nick"). Both wearing navy blue work shirts and work boots. Shot from behind, mid-distance, or with faces turned away — never close-up portraits. Never show full faces clearly.
Setting: Melbourne inner-north — Victorian and Edwardian terrace houses, weatherboard homes, leafy streets, period-style bathrooms and kitchens.
Avoid: Work vehicles/vans, brand logos, close-up faces, stock photo poses, American-style homes, overly staged lighting.
Colour temperature: Warm natural light, slightly golden hour. Complements the Kovele blue (#3d9be9) and gold (#C8961E) palette.
```
